Reads the standard register columns: Employee Name, Designation, Employee ID, Date of Joining, Pay Days, UAN Number, Basic, Gross, P.Tax
How the figures are worked out
Basic = taken from the register; if blank, 50% of Gross.
Special Allowance = Gross − Basic.
PF Employee = 12% of Basic  |  PF Employer = 13% of Basic (rounded to the nearest rupee).
ESIC Employee = 0.75% of Gross  |  ESIC Employer = 3.25% of Gross (rounded up to the next rupee, as the ESIC rules require — this replaces the manual "+1" adjustments in the register).
P.Tax = West Bengal slab, filled in automatically: up to ₹10,000 → 0  |  10,001–15,000 → 110  |  15,001–25,000 → 130  |  25,001–40,000 → 150  |  above 40,000 → 200. You can overtype it.
Net Pay = Gross − PF Employee − ESIC Employee − P.Tax.
Monthly CTC = Gross + PF Employer + ESIC Employer.
PF / ESIC columns start from your register (a 0 there means the person is exempt) and otherwise from the statutory test — PF when a UAN exists, ESIC when Gross is ₹21,000 or less. Untick the box for anyone who should not be deducted.
Pay Days shows as a plain number of days — no "8 / 26".
Every amount on the payslip is a whole rupee, and Net Pay always equals the printed Gross minus the printed deductions.
